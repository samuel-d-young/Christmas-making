# Christmas making

Files and patterns for Christmas craft projects.

## Projects

- **[stocking-template](stocking-template/)** — laser-cut Christmas stocking
  outlines for a Glowforge Aura on a 300 × 300 mm plywood sheet. Three
  layouts: one large stocking, a nested pair, and six smaller ornaments.
  Use them as wooden tracing templates for fabric stockings, or cut them as
  finished wooden stockings for kids to paint and hang.
- **[door-sign](door-sign/)**: "Merry Christmas from the Youngs" front-door
  sign in elegant script, for the same Glowforge Aura and 300 × 300 mm sheet.
  A two-layer holly wreath: the lettered detail glues onto a plain backing
  disc.
- **[earrings](earrings/)**: two-layer green and red Christmas tree earrings
  (star topper, plus four other ideas) and a car-with-tree keyring, with one
  cut sheet per colour.
- **[name-hangers](name-hangers/)**: "Zac" and "Jake" layered name hangers
  for the wall, red script on a green backing.
