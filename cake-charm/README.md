# "the Van Middelkoops" cake charm

A flat script name that presses onto the front of the cake. It's in the same
Great Vibes script as the door sign and the Zac and Jake hangers.

| Layout | File | Size |
|---|---|---|
| One line | [`svg/charm-one-line.svg`](svg/charm-one-line.svg) | 125 × 19 mm |
| Stacked: a small "the" resting on the *V*, like the overlapping "Happy Birthday" in the reference cake | [`svg/charm-stacked.svg`](svg/charm-stacked.svg) | 125 × 30 mm |

- **One piece.** Every word sits on a fine swash underline, so the charm
  goes on in one go and nothing gets lost in the icing. The *i* dots are
  tied to their letters.
- **Size.** 125 mm wide suits the front of a 6–8" cake. Change `CHARM_W` in
  `generate_cake_charm.py` and rerun to resize it; everything scales together.
- **Lines.** Everything is `#FF0000`: set it to **Cut**. There are no score
  or engrave lines.
- **Strength.** The script's hairlines are thickened slightly so they
  survive in **2–3 mm acrylic**. Handle it by the underline, not the
  loops.

## Cutting it in acrylic

The gold look in the reference photo is **gold mirror acrylic**. A
Glowforge Aura is a diode laser: it **cannot cut clear or mirror acrylic**,
and struggles with many acrylic colours. The options are:

- Send this SVG to a laser-cutting service or someone with a CO₂ laser, and
  ask for gold mirror acrylic. The file is ready to cut as-is.
- Or cut it on the Aura in a material it handles, e.g. 3 mm plywood or
  basswood, then paint it gold. Test-cut a scrap corner first.

## On the cake

Peel any protective film off, wash and dry the acrylic, and press it gently
into the buttercream once the cake is iced and chilled. The underline gives
it a straight edge to line up level. It sits flat against the side, so it
needs no stakes.
